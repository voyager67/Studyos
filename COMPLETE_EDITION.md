# StudyOS Complete Edition — Included Capabilities

This repository is presented as one full, integrated edition rather than a sequence of partial feature drops.

Included in the complete build:

- Daily dashboard, task CRUD, due dates, priorities, and weekly planning.
- Syllabus/subject tracking, completion metrics, and subject exam dates.
- Focus timer, task-linked time logs, analytics, streaks, and subject breakdown.
- Calendar, events, deadlines, assessments, recurring time blocks, and `.ics` export.
- Availability-aware 21-day smart planning with focus-sized task splitting.
- Spaced-repetition revision queue and flashcard decks/study sessions.
- Exam/assessment tracking, weighted grades, and target-score estimates.
- Searchable notes vault with tags, pins, safe URLs, and Markdown export.
- Habit check-ins, consistency heatmap, measurable goals, and milestones.
- Command palette, light/dark theme, opt-in browser reminders, complete JSON backup/restore.
- Responsive layout, installable/offline app-shell support, MIT license, and automated quality checks.

The scheduler and review interval logic are transparent rule-based heuristics. StudyOS does not call a remote AI model or backend.
