About StudyOS

StudyOS is a student-built study-planning prototype designed to make planning more realistic and progress easier to see.

S
StudyOS Complete Edition

Smarter study planning. More room to learn.

The planner uses transparent, rule-based scheduling—not an AI model. It does not connect to your college, LMS, calendar, or academic records. Your data is stored locally in your browser.
